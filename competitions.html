Show Your Skills

A1 Kitchen runs eating and cooking competitions. Register online with your photo, get your competitor ticket with a QR code, and compete for prizes. The 1st and 2nd place winners are awarded a prize, and winners receive a certificate that anyone can check by scanning its QR code. Want to watch instead? Get your ticket and come and cheer.
