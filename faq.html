Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about ordering, paying, delivery, refunds and catering. Press a question to open the answer.

Ordering and Paying

What kind of food does A1 Kitchen prepare?

A1 Kitchen prepares both African and European dishes. See the Menu page for the foods you can order.

Can I order food online?

Yes. Open the Menu page, choose your food, and send your order request. You receive an order number and a tracking link.

How much will my order cost?

A1 Kitchen confirms the price of your order with you, by phone, after you send your request. You pay only after you agree to the price.

How do I pay?

You pay the full amount with Orange Money or Afrimoney, then upload a screenshot of your payment. A1 Kitchen checks it before preparing your order.

Does A1 Kitchen ask for my PIN or password?

No. Never share your Orange Money or Afrimoney PIN or password with anyone. A1 Kitchen only needs a screenshot of your payment.

When is my food prepared?

Your food is prepared after your payment has been checked and confirmed.

Delivery and Refunds

How much is delivery?

The delivery fee depends on your location, because transport costs differ across Sierra Leone. A1 Kitchen tells you the fee before you pay.

Can I get a refund?

You can cancel an order request at any time before you pay. If you change your mind after paying, contact A1 Kitchen immediately. If your food has not been prepared yet, your money is refunded. Once food has been prepared, it cannot be refunded. If you pay and never receive your food, A1 Kitchen refunds you. A1 Kitchen does everything it can to make sure you receive every order you have paid for.

What if I will not be at home when my order arrives?

Tell A1 Kitchen immediately, so we can arrange things with you. Please also give a clear and correct delivery address. A1 Kitchen is not responsible for a failed delivery caused by a wrong address or by nobody being available to receive the order.

Catering and Contact

Do you provide catering services?

Yes. A1 Kitchen can be hired to prepare food for any type of event, anywhere in Sierra Leone. Contact A1 Kitchen to discuss your event.

Do you cater for large events?

Catering arrangements depend on the event and requirements. Contact A1 Kitchen to discuss your needs.

How can I contact A1 Kitchen?

Visit the Contact page for the available contact information.

Still Have a Question?

We are happy to help. Reach out and we will get back to you.
