Information We Collect

When you place an order

Your full name, phone number, WhatsApp number (if you give one), whether you want delivery or pickup, your delivery location and instructions, your order notes, and the food you ordered.

When you pay

The payment method you choose (Orange Money or Afrimoney) and the payment screenshot you upload as proof of payment. A screenshot may show other information, such as names or phone numbers. If it shows anything unrelated to the payment, you may crop it before uploading, as long as the amount, date and payment status stay visible.

We never ask for your Orange Money PIN, Afrimoney PIN, bank PIN, password or any other secret login information. Please never share these with us or with anyone claiming to represent us.

When you confirm delivery or report a problem

Your order number, your confirmation code, and the description of any problem you tell us about.

When you register for an event

Your name, phone number, number of guests, and any additional information you choose to give us.

If you pay for event tickets, we also keep the payment screenshot you upload, the ticket numbers we issue to you, and a record of when your tickets were used at the gate. Your registrations and tickets are shown on a private dashboard that only people who have your private link can open.

When you register as a guest at a catering event

Your full name, your phone number and a 4-digit PIN that you choose. The PIN is only for finding your tickets on our website, so please never use your mobile money PIN. We store it in a scrambled form that our staff cannot read. Staff can only give you a new PIN. We also keep your meal tickets and a record of when each meal was served.

Photos, videos and customer words

If we show a photo, video or comment from a customer on our website, we do so with that person's permission.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to prepare and deliver your order (or arrange pickup), check your payment, contact you about your order, confirm delivery, deal with problems or complaints, process event registrations, and provide customer support.

We may also use it where reasonably necessary to keep our business records, protect our website and services, and prevent fraud or misuse. We do not use your information for unrelated purposes.

Who Can See Your Information

Only authorised Awan Kitchen staff can see your order details and payment screenshots. Delivery staff can see the details they need to reach you, such as your name, phone number and delivery address. Payment screenshots for event tickets can be seen only by the Head Chef, the Manager and the Developer.

We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

To run this website we use trusted technology providers: Netlify for website hosting, and Supabase for our online database and file storage. They handle information for us only to keep the website and our systems working.

These providers may store or process information outside Sierra Leone, including in Europe.

To alert our staff when a new order, payment screenshot, catering request or problem report arrives, we use an automation service and SMS and WhatsApp messaging services. These alerts contain only a short count, such as "2 new orders", and never your name, phone number or order details.

Other websites and apps

Some links, such as WhatsApp, take you to other services. Those services have their own privacy policies, and Awan Kitchen does not control them.

Information Saved on Your Device

To make ordering easier, your browser saves your food selection and details of your current order, such as your order number and confirmation code, on your own device.

You can remove this at any time by clearing your browser data. On a shared phone or computer, we recommend clearing it after you finish.

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. For example, access to orders and payment screenshots is limited to authorised staff who sign in to a protected area.

No website or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please do not send PINs or passwords through our website or any of our communication channels.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep order, payment, event and related records for as long as reasonably necessary to run the business, keep proper records, resolve problems or disputes, and protect against fraud or misuse. The time can differ depending on the type of information.

Your Choices

You can contact us to ask what information we hold about you, to ask us to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. We may need to keep some records where this is reasonably necessary for the business, for security, to resolve disputes, or because the law requires it.

Children

This website is not designed for children, and we do not knowingly ask them for unnecessary personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us so we can review it.

The Law

Awan Kitchen aims to handle personal information responsibly. Sierra Leone's Cyber Security and Crime Act 2021 makes it an offence to access computer systems, or to intercept or interfere with data, without authorisation.

Sierra Leone is also working on a dedicated data protection law. We will review and update this policy when new legal requirements apply.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services, website features or the law change. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
