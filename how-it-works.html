How It Works

Ordering from A1 Kitchen takes a few simple steps. Here is everything you need to know, from choosing your food to receiving it.

Ordering and Paying, Step by Step

Step 1

Choose your food

Open the Menu page and press "Add to Order" on the foods you want. You can change quantities or remove items before you continue.

Step 2

Enter your details

Give your name and phone number, and choose delivery or pickup. For delivery, tell us where to bring your order. Add any special requests in the notes.

Step 3

Get your order number

When you submit, your order is recorded and you see your order number and total. Keep your order number safe.

Step 4

Pay with Orange Money or Afrimoney

Choose your payment method, send the exact total to the official A1 Kitchen number shown on the payment page, then upload a screenshot of your payment.

Step 5

We check your payment

A1 Kitchen checks your screenshot before preparing your order. Usual time: this will be added soon.

Step 6

We prepare your order

Once your payment is confirmed, your food is prepared and sent for delivery or made ready for pickup.

Paying Safely

  • Only pay to the official A1 Kitchen number shown on the payment page. Never pay to an unverified number.
  • A1 Kitchen will never ask for your Orange Money PIN, Afrimoney PIN or password. Never share them with anyone.
  • We only need a screenshot of your payment. You do not need to send a transaction ID.
