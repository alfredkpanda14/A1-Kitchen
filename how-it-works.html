Ordering Guide

How It Works

Ordering from A1 Kitchen takes a few simple steps. Here is everything you need to know, from choosing your food to receiving it.

View the Menu Read the FAQ

Ordering and Paying, Step by Step

1

Choose your food

Open the Menu page and press "Add to Order" on the foods you want. You can change quantities or remove items before you continue.

2

Send your order request

Give your name and phone number, and choose delivery or pickup. For delivery, tell us where to bring your order. Then send your request.

3

Get your order number

You receive an order number and a private tracking link. Keep them safe. You can use the link to follow your order at any time.

4

Agree your price

A1 Kitchen contacts you on your phone with the price of your order and, for delivery, the delivery fee for your location. You pay nothing until you agree.

5

Pay in full

Pay the full amount with Orange Money or Afrimoney to the official A1 Kitchen number, then upload a screenshot of your payment.

6

We check and prepare

A1 Kitchen checks your payment. Usual time: this will be added soon. Your food is prepared only after your payment is confirmed, and then delivered or made ready for pickup.

After your order arrives

Open your tracking link and press "I have received my order" (or "I have collected my order" for pickup). If anything is wrong, use the "Something wrong with your order?" form on the same page, or contact A1 Kitchen directly.

Payment, Refunds and Safety

Payment, refunds and your satisfaction

  • Full payment is needed before your food is prepared.
  • You can cancel an order request at any time before you pay.
  • If you change your mind after paying, contact A1 Kitchen immediately. If your food has not been prepared yet, your money is refunded.
  • If you pay and never receive your food, A1 Kitchen refunds you.
  • If you will not be at your delivery location, tell A1 Kitchen immediately. A1 Kitchen is not responsible for a failed delivery caused by a wrong address or by nobody being available to receive the order.
  • Customer satisfaction is one of our key goals. A1 Kitchen does everything it can to make sure you receive your order.

Paying safely

  • Only pay to the official A1 Kitchen number shown on the payment page. Never pay to an unverified number.
  • A1 Kitchen will never ask for your Orange Money PIN, Afrimoney PIN or password. Never share them with anyone.
  • We only need a screenshot of your payment. You do not need to send a transaction ID.
