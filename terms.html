Last updated: 7 October 2026

These terms explain the rules for ordering food and using the website of A1 Kitchen. By placing an order or using this website, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not place an order.

1. Orders

When you submit an order on this website, you are asking A1 Kitchen to prepare and supply the food you selected. We start preparing your order after your payment has been checked and confirmed.

Please check your order, your phone number and your delivery location carefully before you submit. You are responsible for the accuracy of the information you give us.

2. Menu, Prices and Availability

Prices are shown in Sierra Leonean Leones (NLe). The total for your order is calculated by A1 Kitchen from our current menu when you submit your order, and that total is the amount you must pay.

Some foods may sometimes be unavailable, and prices may change. The pictures on the menu show the dish as closely as possible, but the actual presentation may vary slightly.

3. Payment

A1 Kitchen accepts payment by Orange Money and Afrimoney. Please send the exact total of your order to the official A1 Kitchen number shown on the payment page, and then upload a screenshot of your payment. Charges made by your mobile-money provider are separate and are not controlled by A1 Kitchen.

A1 Kitchen checks each payment screenshot. If a screenshot is unclear, does not match your order, or does not show a completed payment, we may reject it and ask you to send a new one.

A1 Kitchen will never ask for your PIN or password. Never share these with anyone. Only pay to the official number shown on our payment page.

4. Delivery and Pickup

You can choose delivery or pickup when you order. The areas we deliver to, delivery fees and delivery times are as confirmed by A1 Kitchen (see the How It Works page).

For delivery, please give a clear delivery location and keep your phone available. If we cannot reach you or find the location, delivery may be delayed.

5. Changes, Cancellations and Refunds

If you need to change or cancel an order, please contact A1 Kitchen as soon as possible. Whether an order can be changed, cancelled or refunded depends on whether preparation has already started. A1 Kitchen will review each request fairly.

6. Problems With Your Order

If something is wrong with your order, such as missing items or a quality problem, please contact A1 Kitchen as soon as possible and tell us your order number and what went wrong. We will review the problem and try to resolve it fairly.

7. Food Allergies and Dietary Needs

If you have a food allergy or dietary requirement, please tell us in your order notes and contact A1 Kitchen before ordering. Our kitchen handles many ingredients, so we cannot guarantee that any food is free from allergens.

8. Catering and Event Bookings

The details of catering for events, including the date, menu, price and payment, are agreed directly with A1 Kitchen.

9. Using This Website

Please give true information, and do not submit false orders or false payment screenshots. Orders or payment proof that appear false may be rejected and, where appropriate, we may take further action.

11. Your Privacy

We handle your information as explained in our Privacy Policy.
