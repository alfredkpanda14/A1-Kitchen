Prices are in Sierra Leonean Leones (NLe). We confirm the price of your order with you, including any delivery fee, before you pay. Where a price is shown on the menu, that price applies to your order.

Some foods may sometimes be unavailable, and prices may change. The pictures on the menu show the dish as closely as possible, but the actual presentation may vary slightly.

3. Payment

Full payment must be made before your order is prepared. We accept payment by Orange Money and Afrimoney. Please send the full amount to the official number shown on the payment page, and then upload a screenshot of your payment. Charges made by your mobile-money provider are separate and are not controlled by Awan Kitchen.

We check each payment screenshot. If a screenshot is unclear, does not match your order, or does not show a completed payment, we may reject it and ask you to send a new one.

We will never ask for your PIN or password. Never share these with anyone. Only pay to the official number shown on our payment page.

4. Delivery and Pickup

You can choose delivery or pickup when you order. The delivery fee depends on your location, because transport costs differ across Sierra Leone. We tell you the fee before you pay.

For delivery, please give a clear and correct delivery location and keep your phone available. If you will not be at the delivery location, or your address changes, tell us immediately. Delivery areas and times are as confirmed by us (see the How It Works page).

Awan Kitchen is not responsible for a failed or delayed delivery caused by a wrong or incomplete address, a location that cannot be found, or you (or the person you named) not being available to receive the order.

5. Cancellations and Refunds

Before you pay: you can cancel an order request at any time by contacting us. You pay nothing.

After you pay: an order that has been paid for is not refunded, except as explained below.

If you change your mind after paying: contact us immediately. If we have not yet prepared your food, your money will be refunded. If your food has already been prepared, it cannot be refunded.

If you do not receive your order: we do everything we can to make sure you receive every order you have paid for. If, even so, you pay and never receive your food, we will refund the amount you paid. Please contact us with your order number as soon as possible so we can look into it.
