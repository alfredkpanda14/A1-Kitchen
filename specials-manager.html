How it works

  • Pick up to 6 foods. They show in a "Today's Specials" band near the top of the home page.
  • Add a short note if you like, such as "Today only" or "Weekend deal". Choose the last day it should show (up to 30 days ahead).
  • A special stops showing by itself after its last day, or when the food is switched off in the Menu Manager.
  • The price comes from the menu. To change a price, use the Menu Manager.
