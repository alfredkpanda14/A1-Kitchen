How it works

  • Customers rate their order (1 to 5 stars) after it is delivered or collected.
  • New ratings wait here. Approve the ones you are happy to show on the home page. Only the customer's first name is shown.
  • Low ratings (1 or 2 stars) are marked in red. Please call the customer and put things right.
  • The average on the home page counts every rating, not only the approved ones, so it stays honest.
