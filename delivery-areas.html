How it works

  • Customers choose their area on the order page and see the delivery fee straight away.
  • If every food in the order has a price, the customer can pay at once. No need to send a price.
  • Customers can also choose "My area is not listed". Then the order comes to "Needs price" as before and you send the price.
  • Switch an area off to hide it for a while (for example in heavy rain) without deleting it.
