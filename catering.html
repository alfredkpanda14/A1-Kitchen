QR TICKETS

Hosting an Invitation-Only Event?

Ask us about QR food tickets. Your guests scan a QR code, register, and receive their food tickets on their phone, for example breakfast and lunch, or whichever meals your event has.

You can approve each guest, or let everyone who scans get a ticket.
