Safety rules

  • Never ask a customer for their PIN or password. You never need it here.
  • Links and new PINs are sent only to the phone number already on the customer's record. If the person is calling from a different number, do not send it there.
  • Check the name and the event with the caller before you send anything. Every link sent and every PIN change is written in the activity log.
